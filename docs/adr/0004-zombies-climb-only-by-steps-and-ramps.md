# Zombies climb only by steps and ramps, and only drop from ledges

Raised and sunken floors (East Hall's dais, the Power Room's control platform, the Workshop's loading dock, the Yard's sunken garden and the Tunnel's dip) are reached by steps and ramps, and Zombies never step up a ledge or jump. A flight of steps is solid as one smooth ramp under the drawn steps, so nothing trips on a step. A Zombie walks straight at its target only where the floor along the way never rises more than `ZOMBIE_STEP_UP` from one sample to the next, or where whatever stops it (a table, the edge of a ledge, a crate the Player stands on) leaves it within reach to swipe. Otherwise it takes the path nodes, from the nearest node it can walk to, to the nearest node its target can be walked to from. Dropping off a ledge is always fine.

So every raised or sunken floor wider than a Zombie's reach has its own path nodes and an edge up its steps, and the Map's tests check that no two-way edge climbs a ledge and that every open spot of floor can walk to and from a path node. The Player is not held to this: their capsule rides up an edge within its radius of a jump's apex, so anything up to about 1.2 m can be clambered onto with a running jump. Props the Player can get on top of are kept low and narrow enough for a Zombie beside them to swipe them anywhere on top.

We chose this over giving Zombies a step-up or a climb because the floor check and the graph are cheap, and testable in the existing headless sims, and because ledges the Player can scramble up but Zombies can only drop from are what give the Player room to break up a Train. The cost is that every new raised floor needs its path nodes placed by hand, as ADR 0001 already accepts. Decided in [Add more colour, floor clutter and gentle verticality to the Greybox Map](https://github.com/jchevertonwynne/zombies/issues/104).
