# Glossary

**Player**: A human-controlled survivor, viewed in first person. A Run has one Player when solo, or two to four in Co-op, each on their own machine.

**Stance**: How upright a Player is: Standing, Crouched or Prone. Lying on the ground in a Last Stand is not a Stance.
_Avoid_: posture, pose

**Crouch**: To lower into the Crouched Stance, or the key that does it.

**Prone**: To lie face-down in the Prone Stance, or the key that does it. A Player in a Last Stand lies on their back and is Down, not Prone.

**Crawl**: To move along the ground while Prone or Down.

**Dive**: Going Prone straight out of a sprint, with a lunge forward.
_Avoid_: dolphin dive, flop

**Co-op**: A Run played by two to four Players together online. Whether a Run is solo or Co-op is fixed by how many Players it starts with, and stays fixed even if some leave.
_Avoid_: multiplayer, online mode

**Host**: The Player whose machine runs a Co-op Run, and who the others join. If the Host leaves for good, the Run ends for everyone.
_Avoid_: server

**Seat**: One of the four places in a Co-op Run, held by one Player for the whole Run. The Host always has the first Seat. A Player who drops and comes back takes their own Seat again, and the Seat sets their spawn point and their colour.
_Avoid_: slot, player number

**Room**: Where Players gather before a Co-op Run, opened by the Host. The Host picks the Map and Mode there, Players take their Seats and ready up, and the party comes back to it after the Run.
_Avoid_: lobby, session, party

**Zombie**: A hostile undead enemy that finds its way to the nearest Player who is up and attacks in melee.

**Round**: One numbered wave of Zombies. A Round ends once every Zombie it contains has been killed. The next Round has a higher number and is harder.

**Health**: The Player's hidden life pool. It refills after a short time without being hit. The screen reddens as it drops.

**Hit Grace**: A short moment after a Zombie's melee hit lands on a Player, during which no Zombie's melee hurts that Player. Explosions, Traps and fire still hurt. It can't be seen. Each Player has their own.
_Avoid_: invulnerability, i-frames

**Speed Tier**: How fast a Zombie moves: Walker, Jogger or Sprinter. It is fixed when the Zombie spawns, and faster tiers become more likely in later Rounds.

**Headshot**: A hit on a Zombie's head. It does more damage and, if it kills, pays more Points than a body kill.

**Horde**: All the Zombies in a Round, both the ones in play and the ones still waiting to spawn. A Round's Horde is bigger than the number allowed in play at once (the Alive Cap). In Co-op the Horde grows with the number of Players at the start of the Round.

**Lost Zombie**: A Zombie the game quietly removes and puts back into the Horde so a Round never stalls on it. It is either wedged, moving less than a metre in 10 seconds, or far from its Player by the way it has to walk (not in a straight line) and not getting any closer for 30 seconds, or for as long as it takes to walk 60 metres if that's longer, so a Crawler gets a minute. A Zombie that keeps crawling after a Player who ran off is not lost: it only counts once they stop and it still isn't closing in. A Crawler stuck at a planked Barrier is lost after 10 seconds, and a Zombie Out of Bounds at once. Zombies that carry over between Rounds, and requested ones, are never lost this way.
_Avoid_: stuck Zombie, cleaned-up Zombie

**Alive Cap**: The most Zombies that can be in play at the same time. The rest of the Horde waits until there is room. Co-op has a higher Alive Cap than solo, the same for any number of Players.

**Train**: A tightly packed group of Zombies following the Player in a line, which the Player leads around the Map so they can be shot from the front.

**Sapper**: A mini-boss on a counter of its own, not part of the Horde, that comes through a Barrier after a warning and walks to a system near the Player to Sabotage it, slamming any Player in its way.
_Avoid_: saboteur, bruiser

**Sabotage**: A Sapper putting a system out of action: the Mystery Box, a working Perk Machine other than Quick Revive, a Trap, the Teleporter, Pack-a-Punch or a Swing Bridge's Levers (all of a bridge's Levers are one system, restored at any of them). A Lock Gate has no Lever, so it can't be Sabotaged. A Sabotaged system stays dead until a Player restores it for Points or the Sapper dies, which brings back everything it Sabotaged.

**Intermission**: The short pause after a Round ends and before the next one begins spawning. On the Polder, the Tide turns during it.

**Weapon**: A gun the Player carries. It has a Magazine and Reserve Ammo.

**Magazine**: The bullets loaded in a Weapon, ready to fire. (Always say "bullets", never "rounds" of ammo, so it doesn't get confused with Round.) Refilled from Reserve Ammo by a Reload.

**Reserve Ammo**: Ammunition a Player carries for a Weapon that isn't loaded yet.

**Knife**: The Player's melee attack. It's always available and separate from Weapons.

**Weapon Class**: The kind of Weapon, which decides how it fires: for example pistol, SMG, shotgun, sniper rifle or launcher.

**Pack-a-Punch**: The machine that, once the Power is on, upgrades the Weapon in the Player's hand for Points. Only the Player who put a Weapon in can take it out.
_Avoid_: upgrade machine

**Upgraded Weapon**: A Weapon that has been through Pack-a-Punch. It has a new name, stronger stats and a glint that marks it out.
_Avoid_: PaP'd gun, camo gun

**Repack**: Paying Pack-a-Punch again to add one random Ammo Mod or Attachment to an Upgraded Weapon. Each Repack of the same Weapon costs more than the last.
_Avoid_: reroll, re-PaP

**Ammo Mod**: An effect on an Upgraded Weapon that has a chance to trigger when it hits a Zombie, then waits out a cooldown. A Weapon holds at most two.
_Avoid_: AAT, alternate ammo type, enchantment

**Attachment**: A part fitted to one mount of an Upgraded Weapon, such as its optic or magazine, that changes how it handles. A Weapon holds at most one per mount.
_Avoid_: mod, add-on

**Underbarrel**: A second gun under an Upgraded Weapon's barrel, with its own ammo, that the Player switches to and fires instead of the main gun. It is either an Attachment or built into the Weapon, like the Masterkey.
_Avoid_: alt-fire weapon, underslung

**Wall Weapon**: A Weapon bought from its chalk outline on a wall. The same chalk sells ammo for that Weapon.
_Avoid_: wall buy, chalk gun

**Box Weapon**: A Weapon that can only be got from the Mystery Box.

**Mystery Box**: A Buyable that sells a random Box Weapon for a fixed price. It sits at one Box Location at a time and moves when it gives a Teddy Bear. Only the Player who spun it can take the Weapon it offers.
_Avoid_: chest, magic box, random box

**Box Location**: One of the fixed places on a Map where the Mystery Box can sit. Every Run starts it at the same one, two or three Doors out of the starting Zone, and none is in the starting Zone itself.

**Teddy Bear**: The spin result that gives no Weapon, refunds the price and sends the Mystery Box to another Box Location.
_Avoid_: joker, bear

**Equipment**: Things the Player carries and uses besides their two Weapons and the Knife: Lethals, Tacticals and Claymores. Equipment never counts toward the Weapon limit.

**Lethal**: Equipment thrown to do damage, such as frag grenades.
_Avoid_: grenade (when meaning the slot)

**Tactical**: Equipment thrown to control Zombies rather than kill them directly, such as the Monkey Bomb.

**Points**: Currency the Player earns by damaging and killing Zombies and spends on Doors and other buys. Each Player has their own and can't give them to anyone else. Not the same as score.

**Buyable**: Anything in the Map the Player spends Points on with the Use key: a Door, Wall Weapon, Mystery Box, Perk Machine, Pack-a-Punch, Trap, Teleporter Pad or Generator. The Power Switch and a Zipline are used but not bought, so neither is a Buyable.
_Avoid_: purchasable, trigger

**Use key**: The single key the Player presses to buy a Buyable, flip the Power Switch or ride a Zipline, or holds to put Planks back on a Barrier.

**Prompt**: The on-screen line saying what the Use key will do here and what it costs.

**Points Earned**: All the Points gained during a Run, before any spending.
_Avoid_: score

**Unpaid Kill**: A kill made by the Map or an effect rather than the Player's own attack, such as a Trap, the Teleporter or a Nuke. It pays no kill Points and drops no Power-up, but still counts toward the Round and the Run Record's Kills.
_Avoid_: free kill, trap kill

**Power-up**: A temporary pickup dropped by a dying Zombie that gives an instant or timed effect to every Player.
_Avoid_: drop, pickup, perk

**Max Ammo**: A Power-up that refills the Reserve Ammo of every Weapon.

**Insta-Kill**: A timed Power-up during which any damage kills a Zombie.

**Double Points**: A timed Power-up during which every Points award is doubled.

**Nuke**: A Power-up that kills every Zombie in play and pays a flat Points bonus in place of kill Points.

**Carpenter**: A Power-up that rebuilds every Barrier and pays a flat Points bonus.

**Fire Sale**: A timed Power-up during which a cheap Mystery Box appears at every Box location.

**Spawn Point**: A fixed place in the Map where Zombies enter play. Most sit just outside one Barrier, and a Zombie that spawns there has to get in through that Barrier. The rest are Risers and Wading Points.

**Barrier**: A planked-up window in a Zone's outer wall. Zombies tear the Planks off to climb in, and can swipe the Player through the window while they do it. The Player can never pass through a Barrier, whether it is planked or not.

**Sill**: The solid wall under a Barrier's window, about waist high. Zombies climb up over it to get in. It stops shots, blasts and thrown Equipment like any wall, and the Planks cover only the opening above it.

**Plank**: One piece of wood on a Barrier. A Zombie tears Planks off one at a time, and the Player earns Points for putting them back. A Barrier is only open to Zombies once every Plank is gone.
_Avoid_: board, plank of wood

**Riser**: A Spawn Point in open ground, where a Zombie climbs up out of the dirt with no Barrier in its way.

**Wading Point**: A Spawn Point under Shallow Water, where a Zombie stands up out of the water with no Barrier in its way and wades ashore, such as in the Cove's surf.
_Avoid_: surf spawn, water Riser

**Map**: The playable space, made of Zones joined by Doors. There are seven: the Greybox, a house round a courtyard with a wing across the field; the Quarry, a pit and its works climbed terrace by terrace; the Village, a long village of streets and lanes with the Power at one end and Pack-a-Punch at the other; the Dam, a road along a dam's crest with galleries inside it and a turbine hall at its foot; the Cove, a cliffside town on four levels (the Shore, the Lower Town, the Upper Town and the Headland), climbed by stairs from the beach to a lighthouse; and the Polder, a canal town behind a sea dyke, its two Canals crossed by bridges and a twin lock with Pack-a-Punch on its island; and the Sanatorium, a hospital on a fogbound hill whose wards and corridors ring a garden court, its three Wings sealed in turn by the Quarantine. The Player picks one beside the Mode before a Run, or the Host does in the Room. The first prototype used a single open Arena.

**Terrace**: One of the Quarry's level shelves, each a storey above the last: the pit floor, then the Rail Yard and the Crusher Shed, then the Winding House and the Offices. The Headframe's deck stands a storey above the top one. Off the pit's west side, the Incline climbs past both terraces with a landing at each, a Door off each into the terrace's building.

**Zone**: A named area of a Map, such as a room or a yard. Each Zone is either open or closed to the Player. Opening a Zone (usually by buying a Door) activates its Barriers and Spawn Points. Zones decide where Zombies come from, not how they move.

**Material Set**: What a Zone's floor and walls are made of (planks and damaged plaster, say), and the decals they wear, such as leak streaks or oil stains. Neighbouring Zones differ by family of material more than by colour. Trim stays in the Zone's paint, and a Zone without a Material Set, or whose files are missing, is drawn in its flat palette.
_Avoid_: theme, skin

**Finish**: What a part of a Weapon's model is made of: gunmetal, blued steel, worn wood, olive paint, brass, tape, leather glove, olive wool sleeve, or plain. Each part carries one, and its UVs point at that Finish's tile in the shared weapon atlas. An Upgraded Weapon's metal and wood show the purple camo instead. With the atlas missing, every part keeps its flat vertex colour.

**Look**: How a Map is lit and graded: its sun, ambient, sky and fog colour, and how strong its lamps are. The Greybox is at dusk.
_Avoid_: time of day, lighting preset

**Active Zone**: A Zone any Player is in, or an open Zone next to one, counting Players in a Last Stand but not those waiting to Respawn. Only Spawn Points in Active Zones send in Zombies, and of those only ones near enough a Player for the Zombie to get there, whenever any is.

**Door**: A barrier between two Zones, or shutting off part of one, that the Player buys with Points to open for the rest of the Run. Zombies never open Doors.
_Avoid_: debris, gate

**Power Door**: A Door that cannot be bought and opens on its own when the Power comes on, such as the Headframe's at the foot of its stairs, or the Village's gates at either end of the Back Lane.

**Arena**: An enclosed, open area with no doors or separate rooms.

**Prop**: Something standing on a Map's floor that is neither a wall nor a Buyable, such as a crate, a table, a pillar or a heap of rubble. The Player weaves round it or climbs onto it; Zombies go round it.
_Avoid_: clutter, cover, obstacle

**Raised Floor**: Part of a Zone's floor built up above the rest, such as a dais, a stage or a dock, reached by steps or a ramp.
_Avoid_: stage, dais (for the general term)

**Infill**: A solid block of the building standing inside a Zone against its walls or in a corner, floor to ceiling, such as a chimney breast, a boxed-off stair core or cupboard, a pier between windows, or a nib that makes an alcove. It wears the room's walls, skirting and cornice, so it reads as part of the building rather than a Prop. The Zone stays a box; an Infill only makes part of it solid.
_Avoid_: pillar (a Prop), wall chunk, blocker

**Ledge**: An edge where one floor stops above another, too high to step up: the side of a Raised Floor or a sunken floor, the Balcony's drop, or the edge of a Terrace, one of the Incline's landings or the Headframe's deck. Zombies only ever drop off a Ledge; the Player can also scramble back up a low one.

**Out of Bounds**: Anywhere outside the Map's playable space: past the edge of its plan, in Deep Water, or fallen well below its lowest floor. A Player past the plan or below the floor goes Down and is put back where a teammate can reach them. A Player in Deep Water is only Washed Up. A Zombie Out of Bounds goes back into the Horde, and a special is moved to a fresh spawn point.
_Avoid_: OOB, the void, kill plane

**Shallow Water**: Water that isn't moving, less than about waist deep, such as the Cove's surf. Players and Zombies wade through it more slowly, and a Player can't go Prone in it.
_Avoid_: puddle, wading water

**Deep Water**: Water that isn't moving, at least waist deep, such as the sea off the Cove's beach. On the Polder, the Tide decides which water is Deep. It stops Players and Zombies like a wall, and anything that ends up in it anyway is Out of Bounds (a Player is Washed Up from it, never Downed). Its banks are lined with foam so it reads before you reach it. Nobody swims and nobody drowns.
_Avoid_: the sea (for the general term), swimming water

**Tide**: The water level of a Map's Canals, which turns one phase each Intermission, through Low, Rising, High and Falling and back to Low. A Run always starts at Low. Only the Polder has one.
_Avoid_: water level, flood

**Low Tide**: The Tide's phase in which a Canal's bed can be waded, slowly, as a shortcut.

**Rising Tide** and **Falling Tide**: The Tide's two middle phases, at the same level. Only the edges of a Canal can be waded, and its middle is Deep Water. They differ only in which way the Tide turns next.

**High Tide**: The Tide's phase in which every Canal is Deep Water from wall to wall, so only bridges and Lock Gates cross it.

**Turn**: The few seconds in an Intermission when the Tide moves to its next phase, after bells warn of it. The water's rules change only when the Turn ends.

**Canal**: Water whose level the Tide moves, all of it together. Its Fords are the stretches that can be waded when the Tide is low enough.

**Ford**: A stretch of a Canal with a berm along each wall and a channel between, where the water is shallow enough to wade at some phases of the Tide.

**Washed Up**: Moved by the Tide to the nearest ground a Player can stand on, with no harm and no Down: a Player in Deep Water, whether the Tide just turned it Deep or they ended up in it any other way, or on a Lock Gate's top or in its sweep as it starts to Swing (to the nearer landing of the Rest they were on).

**Run**: One game, from Round 1 until Game Over or quitting.
_Avoid_: game, match

**Down**: A Player's Health reaching zero, or the Player getting Out of Bounds. Solo, a Down ends the Run unless the Player holds Quick Revive, in which case it starts a Last Stand. In Co-op, every Down starts a Last Stand.

**Last Stand**: The time after a Down when the Player crawls with the Last Stand pistol and cannot be hurt, while Zombies go after anyone else. Solo, it is short and ends in a Self-Revive. In Co-op, it ends in a Revive or a Bleed Out, and the end of a Round doesn't stop it.
_Avoid_: downed state

**Self-Revive**: Getting back up alone at the end of a solo Last Stand.

**Revive**: Getting a teammate in a Last Stand back on their feet in Co-op, by holding the Use key beside them. Being hit doesn't stop it, but letting go does. The revived Player gets their Weapons back.

**Bleed Out**: A Co-op Last Stand running out of time with no Revive. The Player loses everything but their Points, and the other Players lose some of theirs. They watch a teammate until the next Round, then Respawn.

**Respawn**: Coming back at the start of the next Round after a Bleed Out, next to a teammate, with the Player's own starting Weapon, Knife and Equipment (their Kit's, in a Gauntlet Run).

**Game Over**: The end of a Run. Solo, a Down without Quick Revive triggers it. In Co-op, it comes the moment no connected Player is still up, whatever Last Stand time is left. It shows the Round reached and the Run Record.

**Run Record**: The saved summary of one finished Run: Map, Mode, date, Round reached, Kills, Headshots, Downs, Points Earned and time survived. A Gauntlet Run Record also lists the Boons held at the end, in the order they were picked, how many Upgrade Track Ranks were switched on, whether it reached its Win, and its Heat and Heat Conditions. In Co-op each Player saves their own Run Record when their Run ends for them, with their own Kills, Headshots, Downs, Revives and Points Earned, and their teammates listed beside them.

**Records**: The best ten Run Records for a Map, Mode and number of Players at the start of the Run, ranked by Round reached and then by shorter time survived. Gauntlet Records rank Wins above other Runs, then by higher Heat, before Round reached and time.
_Avoid_: high scores, leaderboard

**Lifetime Stats**: Running totals across every Run the Player has finished, solo and Co-op alike. A Co-op Run adds only that Player's own share.

**Settings**: The Player's saved preferences: sensitivity, volume, field of view, invert-Y, camera sway, and an On/Off switch per Special Zombie type (all On by default).

**Developer Mode**: The testing option, always available in a Run and silent until a Cheat is used. Solo it is always allowed. In Co-op the Host sets it in the Room to Off, On (every Player) or Host only, and can change it during the Run. A Run that has used it is Tainted.
_Avoid_: debug mode, god mode

**Cheat**: One action in the Cheat Panel. A toggle stays on for the Player who set it (invincibility, free purchases, infinite ammo, noclip). A one-shot acts at once (Round jump, kill all, spawn a Zombie, grant a Power-up, Weapon, Perk, Boon or Pact, open all Doors, Power on, extra Points). In Co-op a Cheat is a request the Host judges.

**Cheat Panel**: The floating panel of buttons that opens and closes with the backtick key, with the cursor freed while the world keeps running. It shows nothing in a normal Run until it is opened. Under Host only, a client's panel opens with every button disabled.

**Tainted Run**: A Run in which a Cheat has been used. It stays Tainted to the end, even if the Cheat is switched off, and in Co-op it is Tainted for every Player, a Rejoiner included. It writes no Run Record, no Records, no Lifetime Stats, no Commendations and no Scrip, and its Game Over says "Not recorded (developer mode)".
_Avoid_: cheated Run, dev Run

**Info Screen**: The game-info screen shown while the Info control (Tab by default) is held: Map, Mode, Heat, Round, time survived, and a row per Player with their Points, Kills, Headshots and Downs, and in Co-op their Revives. Once the Run is Tainted it adds one line saying so.

**Perk**: A lasting upgrade the Player buys from a Perk Machine. It lasts until the Player's next Down, which takes away every Perk they hold.
_Avoid_: perk-a-cola, buff

**Perk Machine**: The machine on the Map that sells one particular Perk.

**Juggernog**: A Perk that raises the Player's maximum Health.

**Speed Cola**: A Perk that makes Reloads faster.

**Double Tap**: A Perk that makes Weapons fire faster.

**Quick Revive**: A Perk. Solo, it lets the Player get back up from one Down instead of reaching Game Over. In Co-op, it only makes the Player's Revives of others faster.

**Power**: A state of the whole Map that starts off and, once turned on, stays on for the rest of the Run. Most Perk Machines, every Power Door, every Trap and every Jump Pad wait for it. A Map with Generators has no Power and no Power Switch.

**Power Switch**: The switch on the wall that turns Power on. It is free to use. (Not a Lever.)

**Generator**: A Buyable machine that Powers its Section while it is Held. It starts Unclaimed, is Held once a Player Claims it, and is Lost if a Sapper takes it. Only the Dam has them.
_Avoid_: power node, objective, capture point

**Section**: A group of Zones Powered by one Generator. Not every Zone is in a Section.
_Avoid_: wing, area, district, region

**Powered**: A Section whose Generator is Held. On a Map with Generators, a Perk Machine or Trap that would wait for the Power waits for its Section to be Powered instead, and works only while it is. Pack-a-Punch there works only while every Generator is Held, and a Perk Machine or Trap in a Zone outside every Section never waits.

**Claim**: Taking an Unclaimed Generator, by buying it and then Charging it.

**Charge**: Standing on a Generator's plate, while up, until it is Held. Stepping off or going Down pauses the Charge without losing it, and a Surge comes for as long as it runs.
_Avoid_: hold, capture

**Reclaim**: Taking back a Lost Generator, by Charging it again for no Points.

**Surge**: Extra Zombies that come through a Section's Barriers while its Generator is being Charged. They are not part of the Horde, so they don't hold up the end of the Round, and they drop no Power-ups.
_Avoid_: rush, wave, event

**Unclaimed**: A Generator no Player has Claimed yet.

**Held**: A Generator the Players have Claimed or Reclaimed and that is not Lost.

**Lost**: A Generator a Sapper has taken. Unlike a Sabotaged system, it stays Lost after the Sapper dies, until a Player Reclaims it.
_Avoid_: Sabotaged (for a Generator), unpowered

**Dark**: A Zone with no light but the Players' Weapon Lights and whatever glows: a Zone whose Section isn't Powered, or one that waits for every Generator to be Held.
_Avoid_: unlit, blackout

**Weapon Light**: The torch-wide spotlight every Player carries, always on, on a Map with Dark Zones.
_Avoid_: flashlight, torch

**Shifting**: A Piece of the Map (one or more Leaves, each a solid deck or gate that turns about its own vertical hinge) moving between fixed Rests. Nothing rises, falls or slides, and nothing is carried: a body on a Leaf while it turns is pushed off by it. The Polder's Swing Bridges are the first Pieces, turned by Levers; its Lock Gates are the second, turned by the Tide.

**Rest**: One of the fixed places a Shifting Piece can stand still, each with a yaw for every Leaf and the Zone joins it makes. A Swing Bridge has Span (joins its two banks) and Along (lies along its own bank over the water, joins nothing). Zombies and Active Zones count a Rest's joins only while the Piece stands at that Rest and its gate Door (if it has one) is open.

**Swing Bridge**: A Piece with one Leaf, a deck with rails hinged at one bank, that a Lever turns between Span and Along. It crosses Deep Water. A pen and a gate Door guard its near landing; a gate Door joins no Zones itself.

**Lock Gate**: A pair of gates across one of the Polder's lock chambers, a Piece the Tide turns at each Turn that changes its Rest, with no Lever, no cooldown and nothing to Sabotage. Shut, its two Leaves meet mid-chamber and their top is a 1 m walkway with handrails onto the island, reached through a Power Door wicket; Open, they fold against the chamber walls. The Inland Gate is Shut at Low and Rising, the Sea Gate at High and Falling, so exactly one gate top leads to the island at each phase, and which one alternates. A Player on a gate's top or in its sweep when it starts to Swing is Washed Up to the landing nearer them, and a Zombie there is Tipped.

**Lever**: A Buyable for 500 Points, with no Power and no Co-op scaling, that Swings its bridge to the next Rest. Each bridge has one at each landing, outside the pen, and they share one cooldown. A Lever is not a Trap, so no Trap Boon applies.

**Swing**: One Lever pull's work on a Swing Bridge: a Warning (bell and amber lamps), Waiting while any Player, Down or not, is on a Leaf or in its sweep, the turn itself from Rest to Rest, and the cooldown.

**Tipped**: What a Swing does to a Zombie or Hellhound on a Leaf or in its sweep as the turn starts: it is thrown off into the canal and dies as an Unpaid Kill that counts toward the Round and the Run Record. A Napalm, Shrieker or Nova Crawler dies with no death effect. A Sapper is not killed: it loses a Trap's share of its health and is moved to a fresh spawn point.

**Trap**: A Buyable hazard across a doorway that, once switched on, kills every Zombie that walks into it for a short time and can Down the Player too. It then needs a cooldown before it can be bought again.
_Avoid_: electric fence, zapper

**Jump Pad**: A plate in the floor that, once the Power is on, throws a Player who steps onto it up to a fixed spot on a higher floor, over anything in the way. It costs nothing, nothing steers the throw, and Zombies never use one. Only the Quarry has them.
_Avoid_: launch pad, bounce pad, trampoline

**Zipline**: A cable from a lookout down to a lower Zone that a Player rides by pressing the Use key at its top. It costs nothing, needs no Power, and can only be ridden once the Zone it lands in is open. The ride can't be steered or left early, the Player can't fire while on it, and Zombies never use one. Made for the Cove.
_Avoid_: zip line, flying fox, cable car

**Teleporter**: A Map's set of Teleporter Pads and the Mainframe they send the Player to. Only the Greybox has one. It needs Power and only carries the Player one way, to the Mainframe; Zombies have to walk.

**Teleporter Pad**: A Buyable platform that, once Linked, sends the Player to the Mainframe for Points. It kills the Zombies crowded around it when it fires.
_Avoid_: teleporter (for one pad), portal

**Mainframe**: The machine at the Teleporter's single arrival point. Each Teleporter Pad is Linked by using it after activating that pad.
_Avoid_: core, hub

**Link**: Joining a Teleporter Pad to the Mainframe, by activating the pad and then reaching the Mainframe in time. A Link lasts for the rest of the Run. Linking both pads opens the Door shutting the Gazebo, and Pack-a-Punch inside it, off from the rest of the Yard; that Door cannot be bought, and the Power alone doesn't open it.

**Mode**: The ruleset a Run is played under: Classic or Gauntlet. Every Run has exactly one Mode, chosen before it starts and fixed for the whole Run.
_Avoid_: game type, difficulty

**Classic**: The faithful Mode, played as the original games played it.

**Gauntlet**: The Mode that plays exactly like Classic but offers a Boon after every Round.
_Avoid_: roguelite mode

**Act**: One of the three ten-Round stretches of a Gauntlet Run (Rounds 1–10, 11–20, 21–30). Each Act ends in a Boss Round.

**Boss Round**: A Gauntlet Round built around a boss: the last Round of each Act, and every tenth Round in Endless.

**Elite**: An ordinary Zombie of a Gauntlet Run's Horde, from Round 6, that has one Elite Trait. It pays its kill Points twice and drops Power-ups three times as often. At most three are alive at once; Hellhounds, Special Zombies, Nova Crawlers, Bosses and requested Zombies are never Elites, and Turning one strips its trait.

**Elite Trait**: What marks an Elite, each with its own tint: Armoured (body hits soaked until half its health is gone), Splitter (two Walkers on death), Volatile (a fused blast that hurts only the Player), Regenerating (heals after a quiet spell) or Rallying (Zombies near it move faster).

**Win**: Clearing the Boss Round that ends the third Act. The Player then chooses to end the Run or keep going into Endless; a Run that has reached its Win stays a Win whatever happens after. In Co-op each Player chooses for themselves, on their own Win screen over the running world, within 30 seconds (no choice is keeping going): one who ends the Run leaves with the Win and gives up their Seat, the others keep going, and the Host ending it ends it for everyone, every Player keeping the Win.

**Endless**: The Rounds a Gauntlet Run plays after its Win, when the Player keeps going. Endless keeps the Act rhythm, with a Boss Round every tenth Round and a Pact Offer between, but has no Acts and no further Win.

**Boss Offer**: The Boon Offer that replaces the usual one after a cleared Boss Round, offering only the strongest Boons.

**Heat**: Opt-in difficulty for Gauntlet, put together before a Run from Heat Conditions and unlocked by the first Win. A Run's Heat is the total of its Conditions' ranks, and higher Heat pays more Scrip. A Co-op Run plays at the Host's Heat, for every Player.

**Heat Condition**: One ranked rule that makes a Gauntlet Run harder with no upside, such as dearer prices or faster Zombies.

**Boon**: A lasting upgrade the Player picks from an offer after completing a Round in Gauntlet. Boons are picked, never bought, and unlike Perks they survive a Down and a Bleed Out. In Co-op each Player holds their own.
_Avoid_: skill, buff, upgrade

**Boon Offer**: The choice of Boons shown after each Round in Gauntlet; it can't be skipped, but it can be Rerolled or have a Boon Banished. Solo, the world is frozen until the Player picks one. In Co-op, each Player gets their own during the Intermission, which waits for every pick for a short time and then lets the next Round start; a Player who hasn't picked can do nothing until they do.
_Avoid_: draft, level-up, card pick

**Round Choice**: The last step of a Solo Gauntlet Intermission, after the Boon Offer: two options for the next Round, a safe one and one with a Twist, each possibly carrying a Challenge. Rounds 1 and 2, Boss Rounds and Hellhound Rounds are Plain and have none. It can't be Rerolled or Banished, and in Solo the world stays frozen until it's picked. In Co-op it is a vote: every Player votes, the majority wins, a tie goes to the Host's vote, and no vote by the timeout picks the safe option, with the world running meanwhile. A Challenge is the team's: every Player's kills count, any Player's breach breaks it, and everyone gets the reward.
_Avoid_: round vote, modifier pick

**Twist**: What the Round Choice's second option does to its Round: Fog, Stampede, Swarm, Low Gravity or Drought. Surviving it (clearing the Round) raises the Rarity of the next Offer. No Twist repeats within an Act.

**Challenge**: An optional goal on a Round, tied to a Family (Cold Steel, Clean Shots, Full Mag, Hold the Line, Tight Fist, Blitz). Only Player kills count toward one or break it. It fails the moment it's broken, never on a Down, and failing costs only the reward; meeting it raises the Rarity of the next Offer and guarantees a card of its Family. Meeting a Challenge and surviving a Twist in the same Round also gives an extra pick.

**Reroll**: Paying Points to swap all 3 cards of a Boon Offer or Boss Offer for a fresh draw. Each Reroll in the same Offer costs double the one before, and the price starts over at the next Offer. A Pact Offer can't be Rerolled.

**Banish**: Spending a Banish charge to remove one card's Boon from the Offer and from the Player's pool for the rest of the Run, redrawing its slot at once. A Player starts a Run with one charge and gets another for each cleared Boss Round. A held Boon keeps its Stacks, and a Banished Boon still counts toward its Duo. A Pact Offer can't Banish.
_Avoid_: ban, remove

**Rarity**: How often a Boon turns up in Boon Offers: Common, Rare or Legendary. Each Boon has one fixed Rarity, and Rarity never changes how strong it is.
_Avoid_: tier, quality

**Stack**: One copy of a held Boon. A Boon can be picked again and again, each pick adding a Stack.
_Avoid_: level, rank

**Requirement**: A condition a Boon must meet before it can appear in a Boon Offer, such as holding another Boon.
_Avoid_: prerequisite, unlock

**Proc Boon**: A Boon made of a Trigger and an Effect: whenever the Player does the Trigger, the Effect may fire and is seen working on screen. It belongs to the Player, not a Weapon, so it works with any Weapon, the Knife or a Lethal, and survives a Down. Each Stack makes its Effect bigger, never more likely.
_Avoid_: on-hit boon, passive

**Trigger**: The Player action that can fire a Proc Boon, such as a Headshot kill, a Knife kill or taking a hit.
_Avoid_: condition, event

**Effect**: What a Proc Boon does when it fires, drawn from the Ammo Mod elements (chain shock, burn, knockback, phantom gun, Turned) or a few of Gauntlet's own.
_Avoid_: ability, power

**Proc**: One firing of a Proc Boon's Effect. A Proc's damage never fires another Proc or an Ammo Mod.
_Avoid_: activation, trigger (for the firing itself)

**Pact**: A cursed Boon: stronger than any Legendary but carrying a Drawback. Pacts are offered in place of Boons in the middle Round of each Act, may be skipped, and each can be held only once in a Run, by one Player.
_Avoid_: cursed boon, curse, bargain

**Drawback**: The lasting cost a Pact puts on the Run, such as dearer prices or faster Zombies. In Co-op, a Drawback that changes the world falls on every Player, not just the one who picked the Pact.
_Avoid_: downside, penalty, curse

**Scrip**: The currency Gauntlet Runs earn for the Rounds they complete, kept between Runs and spent in the Armory. In Co-op each Player earns their own, as the same Run would pay them solo. Classic never earns or uses it. Not the same as Points.
_Avoid_: credits, currency, XP

**Unlock**: Buying a Boon or Pact with Scrip so that it can appear in Boon Offers from then on. Every Common and a few Rares are unlocked from the first launch.
_Avoid_: requirement

**Upgrade Track**: A permanent Gauntlet bonus, such as more starting Points, bought one Rank at a time with Scrip. Each Track can be switched off before a Run. A Player's Tracks help only that Player, in their own Run or someone else's.
_Avoid_: talent, mirror, upgrade

**Rank**: One bought step on an Upgrade Track. A Track has a fixed number of Ranks.
_Avoid_: level, stack

**Banish Track**: The sixth Upgrade Track, with two Ranks, each adding one Banish charge to every Run's start.

**Kit**: How a Gauntlet Run starts, picked on the Mode screen before the Run: the Standard Kit (free), or a Family Kit that sets the opening Loadout, counts as one held Boon of its Family for Offers, and gives one small rule. A Family Kit is earned by that Family's Commendation and never costs Scrip. Like Tracks, a Kit helps only its own Player.
_Avoid_: class, loadout, starter pack

**Commendation**: A one-off goal that pays Scrip once, across Runs (a Win, a Set Bonus, no Pact, 100 Elites, and so on), shown by a banner when met and again on Game Over. Any Gauntlet Run counts, Endless included. The six Family Commendations also earn that Family's Kit.
_Avoid_: achievement, trophy, medal

**Armory**: The screen, reached from the Mode screen, where Scrip is spent on Unlocks and Ranks and Upgrade Tracks are switched on or off.
_Avoid_: shop, store, meta screen
