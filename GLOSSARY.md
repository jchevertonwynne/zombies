# Glossary

**Player**: The single human-controlled survivor, viewed in first person.

**Zombie**: A hostile undead enemy that finds its way to the Player and attacks in melee.

**Round**: One numbered wave of Zombies. A Round ends once every Zombie it contains has been killed. The next Round has a higher number and is harder.

**Health**: The Player's hidden life pool. It refills after a short time without being hit. The screen reddens as it drops.

**Speed Tier**: How fast a Zombie moves: Walker, Jogger or Sprinter. It is fixed when the Zombie spawns, and faster tiers become more likely in later Rounds.

**Headshot**: A hit on a Zombie's head. It does more damage and, if it kills, pays more Points than a body kill.

**Horde**: All the Zombies in a Round, both the ones in play and the ones still waiting to spawn. A Round's Horde is bigger than the number allowed in play at once (the Alive Cap).

**Alive Cap**: The most Zombies that can be in play at the same time. The rest of the Horde waits until there is room.

**Train**: A tightly packed group of Zombies following the Player in a line, which the Player leads around the Map so they can be shot from the front.

**Intermission**: The short pause after a Round ends and before the next one begins spawning.

**Weapon**: A gun the Player carries. It has a Magazine and Reserve Ammo.

**Magazine**: The bullets loaded in a Weapon, ready to fire. (Always say "bullets", never "rounds" of ammo, so it doesn't get confused with Round.) Refilled from Reserve Ammo by a Reload.

**Reserve Ammo**: Ammunition a Player carries for a Weapon that isn't loaded yet.

**Knife**: The Player's melee attack. It's always available and separate from Weapons.

**Points**: Currency the Player earns by damaging and killing Zombies and spends on Doors and other buys. Not the same as score.

**Points Earned**: All the Points gained during a Run, before any spending.
_Avoid_: score

**Power-up**: A temporary pickup dropped by a dying Zombie that gives an instant or timed effect.
_Avoid_: drop, pickup, perk

**Max Ammo**: A Power-up that refills the Reserve Ammo of every Weapon.

**Insta-Kill**: A timed Power-up during which any damage kills a Zombie.

**Double Points**: A timed Power-up during which every Points award is doubled.

**Nuke**: A Power-up that kills every Zombie in play and pays a flat Points bonus in place of kill Points.

**Carpenter**: A Power-up that rebuilds every Barrier and pays a flat Points bonus.

**Fire Sale**: A timed Power-up during which a cheap Mystery Box appears at every Box location.

**Spawn Point**: A fixed place in the Map where Zombies enter play. Most sit just outside one Barrier, and a Zombie that spawns there has to get in through that Barrier. The rest are Risers.

**Barrier**: A boarded-up window in a Zone's outer wall. Zombies tear the boards off to climb in. The Player can never pass through a Barrier, whether it is boarded or not.

**Riser**: A Spawn Point in open ground, where a Zombie climbs up out of the dirt with no Barrier in its way.

**Map**: The playable space. The first prototype used a single open Arena; real Maps are made of Zones joined by Doors.

**Zone**: A named area of a Map, such as a room or a yard. Each Zone is either open or closed to the Player. Opening a Zone (usually by buying a Door) activates its Barriers and Spawn Points. Zones decide where Zombies come from, not how they move.

**Active Zone**: The Zone the Player is standing in, or an open Zone next to it. Only Spawn Points in Active Zones send in Zombies.

**Door**: A barrier between two Zones that the Player buys with Points to open for the rest of the Run. Zombies never open Doors.
_Avoid_: debris, gate

**Power Door**: A Door that cannot be bought and opens on its own when the Power comes on.

**Arena**: An enclosed, open area with no doors or separate rooms.

**Run**: One game, from Round 1 until Game Over or quitting.
_Avoid_: game, match

**Down**: The Player's Health reaching zero. A Down ends the Run unless Quick Revive is active.

**Game Over**: The end of a Run, triggered by a Down without Quick Revive. It shows the Round reached and the Run Record.

**Run Record**: The saved summary of one finished Run: Map, date, Round reached, Kills, Headshots, Downs, Points Earned and time survived.

**Records**: The best ten Run Records for a Map, ranked by Round reached and then by shorter time survived.
_Avoid_: high scores, leaderboard

**Lifetime Stats**: Running totals across every Run the Player has finished.

**Settings**: The Player's saved preferences: sensitivity, volume, field of view and invert-Y.
